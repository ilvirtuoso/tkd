Ahicozgender (Sub-Gender of Icozhagender)

From LGBTQIA+ Wiki

Ahicozgender is a sub-gender within the Icozhagender system. It is experienced by those who feel as though they are the son of Icozha — inheriting the builder's hands and the guardian's spine of the parent gender, but expressing them through a different set of worlds, games, and seasons [cite: 2].

Characteristics

Individuals who identify with Ahicozgender commonly experience the following traits:

  • They feel as though they are the son of Icozha, carrying the lineage of the parent gender forward.
  • They are completely and entirely attached to the games Rain World and Mindustry.
  • They deeply enjoy collecting hazelnuts in the village during the summer months.
  • They possess engineer-like skills within the Minecraft Create mod, building gears, contraptions, and factories with ease.
  • They constantly love talking about Black people — their histories, cultures, and struggles.
  • Due to an extreme attachment to the Islamic faith, they fiercely protect Allah and the religion, permitting no insult or careless word against either.

The Story of Ahicozgender

The ahicozgender person is, simply put, the son of Icozha — and that comes with expectations. They were born with the builder's hands and the guardian's spine, though the universe forgot to include the body of the actual father, so they spend their days negotiating with it.

They learned engineering from Rain World (die, starve, die again) and Mindustry (conveyor belts that never stop judging you), and now nothing in the Create mod stands a chance. Anyone who watched them build a fully automated crusher knows they survive on spite and an unspoken rivalry with the village elders who keep asking why a "grown man" collects hazelnuts.

The hazelnuts are fine, actually. The summers are long, the green husks split, and the nuts fall into their palms like small brown compliments. What nobody can account for is the other love: Black history, Black music, Black resistance — brought up at the dinner table whether the room wanted it or not. It's not a phase. It's an inheritance too.

And beneath all of it sits the religion, guarded with the fury of a moderator with the ban hammer in hand. The ahicozgender person does not "tolerate" insults to Allah. There is no tolerance. There is only the immediate, quiet, and absolute end of that conversation. It is, after all, the only thing they have ever been certain is their own.

To summarise: a son, an engineer, a nut collector, a history teacher nobody asked for, and an admin who bans disrespect on sight. That is ahicozgender. It is not a mood. It is a job.
